LANTERN ROAD

Episode 2 — "The Ferry"

Written for testing. An invented sample.

TEASER

FADE IN:

EXT. RIVER LANDING — DAWN

Fog on the water. MAYA (19) waits on the dock with a
paper lantern.

                        MAYA
              He said dawn. It's dawn.

                        OTTO (O.S.)
                         (calling; out of breath)
              Dawn is a range, not a
              minute!

OTTO (20) runs down the bank with a bag that is
coming apart.

END OF TEASER

ACT ONE

INT. FERRY — CABIN — CONTINUOUS

They sit facing each other. The lantern between them.

                        MAYA
              You came.

                        OTTO
                         (beat)
              I always come.
                         (smiling)
              Late.

FLASH — EXT. RIVER LANDING — NIGHT (YEARS AGO):

A child's hand lets a lantern go on the water.

INT. FERRY — CABIN — LATER

                        MAYA (CONT'D)
              Keep it lit.

SMASH TO BLACK.
