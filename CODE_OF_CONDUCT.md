# Code of conduct

haggs is for people who make things. Be the kind of collaborator you would want on your own project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree about the work, not the person.
- Give feedback you could act on yourself, and take feedback without making it a contest.
- Credit people for what they made, and do not pass off someone else's pictures, voices or writing as your own.
- Do not make or share images, video or voices of a real person without their permission. haggs is built to make that
  harder, not easier.

## What we do not allow

Harassment, threats, slurs, sexual content aimed at people who did not ask for it, doxxing, and sustained disruption of
a discussion. The same goes for spam and for using the project to deceive people.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block someone from the repository, when
this code is not followed. To report a problem, message [@HaggsField](https://x.com/HaggsField) on X or use a GitHub
private report. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere you speak for the project.
