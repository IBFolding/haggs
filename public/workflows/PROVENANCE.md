# Haggs custom-node badge

The badge identifies the custom code in the user-supplied Contest Lab, Game Director and HaggsStudio packs. All contain MIT notices naming “Haggs Contest Lab contributors”, Haggs-prefixed node classes, and the corresponding implementation source. This is source-package attribution, not independent verification of contributor identities.

The badge does not claim authorship of ComfyUI, LTX, MiniMax H3, SAM2, or upstream generation graphs. Their attribution and licenses remain in each pack's `upstream` directory. Model weights have separate terms; they are not bundled or licensed by this badge.

The first four LTX workflows are adaptations of Comfy-Org and Lightricks examples. They deliberately do not receive the custom-node badge. Their sources and licenses are in `ltx/SOURCES.json` and the adjacent license files.

Contest Lab: `contest-lab/LICENSE`, `contest-lab/custom_nodes/ComfyUI-HaggsContestLab/`.
Game Director: `game-director/LICENSE`, `game-director/custom_nodes/ComfyUI-HaggsGameDirector/`.
HaggsStudio: `world-motion-vfx/LICENSE`, `world-motion-vfx/custom_nodes/ComfyUI-HaggsStudio/`. The badge covers its custom room, motion bridge and mask/compositing code, not Eden, HY-Motion, H3 or LTX.

All photographic sample images created for these cards are illustrative. They are labeled separately from supplied deterministic synthetic previews. Neither is evidence of a successful LTX/H3 generation run.
