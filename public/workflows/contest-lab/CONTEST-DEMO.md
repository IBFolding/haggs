# Suggested demonstration: make the room play the song

A focused 60–90 second demo:

1. Show a five-second original performance and a song in ComfyUI.
2. Show the LTX or H3 generated ocean/architecture effect layer and its generation node.
3. Draw a portal region in ComfyUI's Mask Editor. Connect a tracked foreground mask so the performer passes in front.
4. Queue Portal Paint. Show the portal and the beat-pulsed edge.
5. Reuse that same take in World Orchestra; connect an RGB region map and show different regions responding to different frequency bands.
6. Change the beat offset, portal region or effect amount in the reuse graph. Show that no new model take is submitted.
7. Briefly show the custom node source, workflow files and diagnostic report.

Explain the innovation as assigning timing and spatial roles to generated imagery while preserving the original performance. Avoid claiming exact generated action synchronization, automatic instrument separation, novel model training, or guaranteed identity preservation.

The H3 path uses the official Comfy partner node. The LTX path uses local open weights. Do not describe H3 partner generation as open weights. A workflow file pack alone does not fulfill the contest's working-app, repository, license, setup and demonstration requirements.
